Our story · La nostra storia

Tradition is not a museum.
It is something we practice.

In November 1877, a small group of Italian immigrants created a mutual-aid society to help one another build new lives in Los Angeles without losing the culture of home.

That spirit still guides us. Garibaldina has evolved from mutual benevolence to a welcoming social and cultural home—one where generations gather, share a meal, dance, volunteer, and keep Italian traditions alive together.

Follow the story ↓
1877

A society is born

La Società di Mutua Beneficenza is organized by Italian immigrants in Los Angeles.

1916

Two communities unite

Los Angeles’s oldest Italian societies merge as Società Garibaldina di Mutua Beneficenza.

1967

Highland Park becomes home

Members build the North Figueroa clubhouse that still welcomes the community today.

2017

The table grows

Membership opens beyond Italian descent, welcoming everyone who values the culture.
